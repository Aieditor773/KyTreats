Bakery Guidelines

Ordering Policies & FAQ

All sales are final. When placing orders, payment must be made in full upfront unless your total order is greater than $60. Orders over $60 require a 50% deposit to secure the calendar date, with the balance due before pickup. Deposits can be sent via Apple Pay (309-415-9636) or Cash App ($KiyYoung2).
Custom cakes, treat boxes, and party packs should be booked at least 1 to 2 weeks in advance. For Weddings, please book at least 1–2 months in advance. For Kids Cake Decorating Parties, you must book at least 1 month in advance.
Standard order pickup hours are between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM in Peoria, IL. If you need a later pickup time outside of these hours, please note your requested time in the order description when booking.
You can paste links to your Pinterest pins, Instagram posts, or Google Drive in the booking form, or text your photos directly to our bakery line at (309) 415-9636 after submitting the form.
